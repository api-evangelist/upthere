# Upthere

> Upthere was a consumer cloud-storage company backed by GV and founded by former
> Apple software engineering chief Bertrand Serlet. Its Upthere Home app was "one
> home" for photos, videos, music, and documents, stored directly in the Upthere
> cloud with fast search, private sharing, and shared "Loops". Western Digital
> acquired Upthere in 2017 and later discontinued the service. upthere.com now
> redirects every path to a retired Western Digital support notice. Upthere never
> published a public developer API, and no API surface remains.
